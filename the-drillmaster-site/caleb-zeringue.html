Co-Creator

Caleb Zeringue

C

Caleb Zeringue writes, produces, and does stand-up. He co-wrote Luigi the Musical, served as its original executive producer, and performed in it — the sold-out show that somehow ended up in The New York Times, CNN, NPR, The Washington Post, and The Hollywood Reporter. Over 3,000 tickets later, it made it to Edinburgh Fringe.

He's a NYC Queer Comedy Festival finalist. Don't Tell Comedy, Cobb's, The Stand, Gotham, Hollywood Improv. He features for Jenny Zigrino (Dropout.TV, Sirius XM) enough that it's basically a part-time job. Queer, southern, a little too honest for his own good.

He and Jenny also made The Drillmaster. It sold out The Elysian in LA and comes back October 13 for another staged reading, with Off-Broadway in the mix. More at calebzeringue.com.
