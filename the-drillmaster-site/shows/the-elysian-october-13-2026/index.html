Part historical fact. Part historical farce.

An army in need of discipline.
A Baron with other ideas.

America is turning 250. Let’s talk about who drilled the men. In 1778, Baron von Steuben helped turn Washington’s struggling army into a fighting force. In our mostly true version, he’s also trying to get promoted, keep his lovers happy, and find a country that will let him stay.

Created by Jenny Zigrino and Caleb Zeringue, and written with Jeffrey Jay, The Drillmaster gives the Founding Fathers military ambitions, relationship problems, and some extremely unprofessional behavior. See the newly revised script performed by a cast of comedians at The Elysian on October 13.

Is this stand-up or a play?

It’s a live staged reading of a new historical comedy. The cast performs the revised script as part of the play’s development.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 7pm. The show starts at 7:30pm, and The Elysian lists a scheduled end time of 8:45pm. Late seating is at the show’s discretion.

How much are tickets?

General admission is currently $35 plus $7.53 in fees ($42.53 total). Tickets are sold by The Elysian; its checkout shows current pricing and availability.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. This performance is for ages 21 and over.

Do I need to know American history?

No homework required. This is a mostly true comedy about the people who helped build America and the desires its history books leave out.
